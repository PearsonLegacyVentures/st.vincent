CHARTER WELLNESS · St. Vincent & the Grenadines

One charter. Multiple treatments. One coordinated enquiry.

Have several yacht guests requesting massage—or crew members seeking recovery after a passage? We can explore a private treatment schedule with one or more qualified therapists, according to confirmed local coverage, safe onboard space and actual availability.

Plan the group, not just one appointment

Tell us how many people require treatment, how long each session should last, whether treatments may run consecutively, and whether you need more than one therapist at a time. Large group requests receive a custom quote rather than an unverified fixed price.

Local ports and multi-island itineraries

For Bequia, Mustique, Canouan and St. Vincent, share the vessel name, captain or purser contact, berth or anchorage, and boarding arrangement. If the yacht sails onward to another Caribbean island, include the dates so our team can explore that destination separately.

Before confirmation: captain approval, local professional eligibility, marina rules, safe treatment space, access or tender transfers, weather and staffing must be checked. We do not advertise guaranteed yacht coverage.
