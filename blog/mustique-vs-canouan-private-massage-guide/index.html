Mustique or Canouan? Planning Private Massage in the Grenadines

A multi-island holiday needs island-specific wellness planning—not a single vague booking address.

← Grenadines Wellness Journal · November 3, 2026

Quick answer: If you are staying on Mustique or Canouan, book massage using the specific island, property name and access instructions. These are different islands with different travel and property arrangements. Therapist availability on one island does not automatically mean a session is available on another.

Why the island matters more than the postal address

St. Vincent and the Grenadines is an archipelago of distinct islands and cays. The St. Vincent and the Grenadines Tourism Authority promotes both Mustique and Canouan as individual destinations. A private villa or resort appointment has to be organized for the island where the guest will actually be staying—not simply marked “SVG.”

Mustique: privacy and property coordination

For a private Mustique villa, the most helpful first step is to ask your host or property manager whether an outside massage therapist can enter. Send your villa name, gate or visitor procedure and a local point of contact. See our Mustique massage service area for location-specific information.

Canouan: resort suites and villa stays

Canouan guests may be staying at a resort, residence or private villa. Hotels sometimes have their own wellness programmes or restrictions on external providers. We do not assume permission; share the property name and have the rules checked before a therapist is confirmed. Learn more about mobile massage in Canouan.

Couples appointments need two things

A couples massage requires a suitable space for two professional tables and two therapists whose availability has been confirmed for that island. For a honeymoon suite, ask whether the living area is large enough; for a villa, a cool indoor room can be more reliable than an exposed terrace.

Choose Swedish, deep tissue or a custom setting

Swedish massage is geared toward relaxation and a slower holiday afternoon. Deep Tissue is firmer, targeted work for ordinary muscle tension. For an outdoor beach-adjacent treatment, ask specifically about beach massage and the access or weather arrangements.

Do not assume inter-island transfers are included

A request for Mustique is not a booking for Canouan, Bequia or mainland St. Vincent. If your itinerary moves between islands, send the exact dates on each island and ask which locations can realistically be served. Ferry, charter or yacht schedules are separate travel arrangements; do not plan a treatment around an unconfirmed transfer.

Details to put in the first request

  • Specific island and property or yacht name
  • Date and preferred time, with a flexible window if possible
  • 60- or 90-minute service and number of guests
  • Whether the property authorizes outside providers
  • Indoor room size, gate access and local contact

FAQs

Can I book two treatments on different islands in the same week?

You can request both, but they must be confirmed separately based on local coverage and therapist availability.

Can a yacht guest book in Mustique or Canouan?

Potentially, subject to a suitable space, vessel access and island-specific therapist coverage. See Grenadines yacht massage.
